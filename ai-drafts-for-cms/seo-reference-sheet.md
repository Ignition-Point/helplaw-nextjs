## Cross-Case SEO Patterns

**All case pages should include the focus keyword in:**
1. The page title / H1
2. The first paragraph of the intro
3. At least one section heading (usually "Do You Have a Case?" or legal rights)
4. At least one FAQ question (phrased as a full search query)
5. The closing CTA section
6. The meta title and meta description

**FAQ questions should be written as full-sentence search queries:**
- BAD: "How much does it cost?"
- GOOD: "How much does it cost to file a Northwell Health hidden camera lawsuit?"
- BAD: "Can a family member file?"
- GOOD: "Can a family member file a nursing home sexual abuse lawsuit in California on behalf of an elderly victim?"

**Secondary keywords should appear naturally in:**
- Section body text (not forced into headings unless they read naturally)
- "What You Need to Know" bullet points
- "Who Is Responsible?" sub-sections
- News/timeline entries
- FAQ answers
